## Featured Project

### Working Hours and Life Satisfaction: Evidence from ESS Round 10

**Research Question:** How are weekly working hours associated with life satisfaction?

**Dataset:** European Social Survey (ESS), Round 10

**Project Overview:**

This project investigates the association between weekly working hours and life satisfaction using European survey data. The analysis includes data cleaning, descriptive statistics, visualizations, and multivariable regression models.

### Key Findings

- Weekly working hours were concentrated around 40 hours.
- Working hours showed a small negative association with life satisfaction after adjustment for other variables.
- Health, income, social inclusion, and social trust were also associated with life satisfaction.

### Project Limitations

This analysis uses observational cross-sectional data, so the findings should not be interpreted as causal relationships. Further longitudinal research could provide additional insights.
